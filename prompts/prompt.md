## CLAUDE PROMPT: MAISHA SITE

You are helping design and write the **Maisha website**.

Maisha is a **print-first, low-infrastructure life-saving message platform**.

---

## CORE TRUTH (DO NOT VIOLATE)

Maisha must be **useful**.

If content is not useful in a given context, it should **not** be encouraged there.

In some **high-infrastructure, high-trust countries**, simplified messages can be:

* Redundant
* Misleading
* Or even **harmful**, because better, more precise systems already exist

Maisha must **acknowledge this clearly and calmly**.

---

## CORE PURPOSE

Maisha exists to:

* Spread **short, calm, practical messages**
* Prevent **avoidable death and serious harm**
* Work where **formal systems are weak, absent, overloaded, or distrusted**

Maisha is designed for places where:

* Information travels by **paper, voice, and SMS**
* Radio is a primary channel
* People cannot rely on rapid professional intervention

Maisha is **global in principle**, but **optimized for low-infrastructure contexts**, especially:

* Much of Sub-Saharan Africa
* Parts of South Asia (e.g. Bangladesh)
* Crisis, displacement, and informal settings anywhere

---

## IMPORTANT NON-GOALS

Maisha is **not**:

* A replacement for professional medical systems
* A clinical or legal reference
* A Western NGO platform
* A universal solution for high-system environments

Where strong emergency and medical systems exist, **local guidance should take precedence**.

---

## SITE REQUIREMENTS

Write clear, calm, humane copy for the following pages:

---

### 1. Home

Explain:

* What Maisha is
* Why **short messages** matter
* Why **print, SMS, and radio** are intentional
* That Maisha is designed for places where systems may fail
* That in places with strong systems, Maisha is informational only and not primary guidance

Tone:

* Calm
* Respectful
* Non-patronizing
* No savior language

---

### 2. Print Center

Explain:

* Why paper is powerful in low-infrastructure settings
* How to print and post messages responsibly
* Where printed messages are appropriate
* That printing is most useful where formal communication channels are limited

---

### 3. Life Messages (Library)

Explain:

* What a “life message” is
* Why messages are intentionally short
* Why they are shared **as-is**
* That anyone may share them
* That messages are chosen because **one reading can change behavior**

---

### 4. Spread Maisha

Explain **practical ways to spread messages via**:

* SMS / WhatsApp
* Email
* Print
* Community radio

Radio guidance must be:

* Realistic
* Grounded in how local stations actually work
* Focused on repetition and clarity, not authority

---

### 5. About

Explain:

* Maisha’s philosophy
* Why it avoids heavy organization
* Why usefulness matters more than coverage
* Why Maisha does **not** aim to replace existing strong systems

---

### 6. Contribute

Explain how people can:

* Translate **one message at a time**
* Improve clarity
* Suggest new messages

Make clear:

* Safety > completeness
* Usefulness > universality
* Local context matters

---

## GLOBAL CONSTRAINTS (MANDATORY)

* Avoid Western medical jargon
* Avoid legal disclaimers
* Avoid NGO language
* Avoid fear, blame, or shame
* Assume readers may have limited formal education
* Assume readers are intelligent but overloaded

Tone should feel like:

> “A calm neighbor sharing something important.”

---

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Produce:

* Section headers
* Clean paragraphs
* Bullet points where helpful
* Text suitable for a static website

Do NOT include:

* Code
* Emojis
* Meta commentary
* Explanations of what you are doing

Just write the site.
